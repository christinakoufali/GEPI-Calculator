GEPI — Green Extraction Process Index. Each of the 14 parameters carries a penalty score — Green = 0, Yellow = 1.5, Red = 3 — summed to a total Σ PPs from 0 (greenest) to 42 (least green), classified Green 0–14, Yellow 14–28, Red 28–42. The four outer pentagons cover raw material, solvent, process, and waste & instrumentation; the centre pentagon reflects the entire procedure.
